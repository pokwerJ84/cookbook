Japan Protein Cookbook v12
==========================

NEW
- Every built-in recipe can now be edited.
- Edited built-in recipes are saved as non-destructive overrides.
- Built-in recipes can be restored to their original version.
- Added automatic nutrition calculation from ingredient quantities.
- Added a servings field for whole recipes and batch baking.
- Added an exact family-ingredient editor.
- Added a quick button for 1 teaspoon of peanut butter.
- Added recognition for grams, kilograms, millilitres, litres, eggs/items,
  tablespoons, teaspoons, fractions and ranges.
- Added nutrition support for all ingredients currently used by the 63 recipes.

CALCULATION BEHAVIOUR
- Existing recipes retain their trusted original nutrition.
- Ingredient edits apply only the nutritional difference.
- Example: Jarda's breakfast remains 523 kcal. Adding 1 tsp peanut butter
  changes it to about 552 kcal, 51.3 g protein, 60 g carbohydrates,
  12.5 g fat and 12.3 g fiber.
- New recipes are calculated directly from all recognized ingredients.
- Automatic calculation can be disabled for manual nutrition entry.

FIXED
- Version numbers are synchronized to v12.
- Ingredient totals no longer remain from a previously opened recipe.
- Family mode uses real derived/replacement ingredient lines rather than
  treating instructions as food.
- Family shopping-list entries use the family ingredients.
- Fractions such as 1/2 tsp, small bananas/apples and item counts are parsed.
- User-entered text is normalized and dangerous HTML characters are removed.
- All 147 unique built-in ingredient lines are recognized by the calculator.

CLOUD COMPATIBILITY
- No new Supabase column is required.
- User recipes and built-in overrides share the existing custom_recipes JSON.
